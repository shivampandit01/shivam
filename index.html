Backstage

I help event agencies get client sign-off before fabrication. My renders show vendors and fabricators what to build, so you can spot problems before they cost time on site.

I work through the whole venue, from the entrance arch and main stage to the aerial floor plan. I model, light and render every layout in Blender or Unreal Engine 5, using Midjourney, ChatGPT, Gemini and Kling only for ideas and visual references.
