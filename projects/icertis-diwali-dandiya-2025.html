Icertis Diwali Dandiya 2025

Traditional Festival · Grand Corporate Scale

Project brief

Full-venue visualization for Icertis's annual Diwali celebration, one of Pune's largest corporate cultural events. The brief called for a traditional yet grand atmosphere for 500+ employees, with every zone visualized in 3D before any structure was commissioned.

Deliverables

A chandelier entrance arch with branded signage, an outdoor walkway with lit pillar installations, the main stage with heritage motifs and LED uplighting, a themed photobooth wall, and a complete venue floor plan.

  • Entrance Arch
  • Walkway Installation
  • Stage Design
  • Photobooth
  • Floor Plan
