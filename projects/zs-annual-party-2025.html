ZS Annual Party 2025

Aqua Theme · Deep Sea Dive

Project brief

Complete visualization for ZS Consultants' annual party around an immersive deep-sea concept. Multiple distinct zones were needed across a large outdoor venue, all fully visualized before build approval.

A glowing ocean-themed registration counter, a dramatic shark-mouth entrance structure, the Atlantis Lounge branded bar, and dual aerial views of the full venue layout. Visuals secured leadership sign-off with zero revision rounds.
