KPIT Exhibition Concept for BMGE 2027

Clean, Tech-Forward Booth · Vehicle Showcase

Project brief

Exhibition booth design for KPIT at Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2027: an open-plan white-and-wood stand built around a showcase vehicle, with clear visitor flow from reception to display wall, meeting room and lounge.

Deliverables

A complete booth layout with a central vehicle display, KPIT-branded reception counter, screen-led display totems, a back-wall demo counter with monitors, an enclosed meeting room and an informal seating lounge, in front and aerial 3D views.
