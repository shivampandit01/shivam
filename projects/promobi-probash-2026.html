Promobi Probash 2026

Hollywood Awards Night

Project brief

End-to-end visualization for Promobi's Probash 2026, a Hollywood-themed awards night covering the full guest journey from arrival to the main hall. Six distinct zones, one cohesive narrative, all 14 renders delivered in 5 working days.

Deliverables

A gold sequin registration backdrop with velvet ropes, a double-height marquee entrance arch, a red-curtain corridor, the main auditorium stage, two photobooth setups, and a complete floor plan.
