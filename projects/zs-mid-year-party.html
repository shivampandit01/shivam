ZS Mid Year Party

High-Energy LED Stage · Outdoor World Theme

Project brief

Stage and outdoor venue visualization for ZS Consultants' Mid Year Party: two distinct visual experiences in one event, a high-energy stage concept and an expansive World Destinations outdoor dining zone.

Deliverables

A geometric LED stage with layered orange and blue accent lighting (close-up and in full seating context), eight destination-themed photobooth panels, and a monumental Arc de Triomphe entrance gate with the ZS logo integrated into the facade.
